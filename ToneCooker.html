    Other tubes you own

    Open a colour group and tick the tubes you have. Recipes will use them when they get closer to your colour.

    How it works: each paint is modelled by how much light it reflects across the spectrum, and mixtures are calculated with the Kubelka–Munk model that paint chemists use. Match quality is ΔE2000: under 2 is hard to tell apart, under 5 is close. Real paints differ by brand and screens differ from paper, so mix a little first, compare it dry next to your target, and adjust.
